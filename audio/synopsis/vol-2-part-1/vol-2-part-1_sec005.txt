This scholarly overview of Chapter Five delves into the pervasive institution of slavery within the historical and legal frameworks of ancient India. The narrative commences by establishing the undeniable existence of slavery in the Vedic times, tracing its roots and presence in the earliest strata of Indian society. Moving into the elaborate legal codifications, the smṛtis provide a comprehensive understanding by meticulously detailing the diverse kinds of slaves, distinguishing them based on various criteria pertinent to their acquisition and status.

Beyond classification, these authoritative texts also systematically address the critical subject of manumission of slaves. They delineate the specific conditions and prescribed procedures through which a slave could attain freedom, thereby transitioning from servitude back to liberty. This detailed exposition, spanning from the historical reality of slavery in Vedic times and its nuanced classification in the smṛtis, to the provisions for emancipation, forms the core of this chapter's comprehensive study.
