=== CHAPTER ===
44. Pitāmaha

=== KANE'S SYNOPSIS ===
Quotations from Pītāmaha occur mostly on vyavahāra, patri- cularly ordeals – he treats of nine ordeals – 50 *chalas* enumerated by Pītāmaha in which king took action without a complaint – views peculiar to Pītāmaha, viz. 18 lowest castes, eight constituents of hall of justice, &c. – mentions Bṛhaspati – flourished between 400-700 A. D.
