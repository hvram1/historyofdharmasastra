=== CHAPTER ===
48. Prajāpati

=== KANE'S SYNOPSIS ===
Prajāpati cited as authority by Baudhayana Dh. S. (II. 4. 15) and Vasistha (III. 47 &c.) – they mean probably Manu – a com- pilation in 198 verses ascribed to Prajāpati – Mit. and Aparārka and others quote Prajāpati on aśauca, prāyaścitta, śrāddha, ordeals and vyavahāra.
