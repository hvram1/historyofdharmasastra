Jātūkarṇya holds a notable position as a dharmaśāstrakāra, as evidenced by a specific verse attributed to Vṛddha Yājñavalkya, which explicitly names him among the esteemed authorities on Hindu law and custom. This direct mention by a figure of Vṛddha Yājñavalkya's stature firmly establishes Jātūkarṇya's recognition within the classical tradition.

Further insight into Jātūkarṇya's contributions is primarily gleaned from quotations of his work found in later legal commentaries. These extensive citations are presented in verse form, indicating that Jātūkarṇya's original compositions were metrical, a common style for authoritative dharmaśāstra texts. His pronouncements are frequently encountered in the seminal Mitākṣarā commentary, an authoritative work itself, and continue to appear in various other subsequent dharmaśāstra treatises. The consistent reproduction of his verses across these foundational texts highlights the enduring relevance and perceived authority of Jātūkarṇya's legal and ethical insights for generations of scholars and jurists.
