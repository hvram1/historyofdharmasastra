=== CHAPTER ===
60. Bhartri-yajna

=== KANE'S SYNOPSIS ===
An ancient Bhāṣyakāra mentioned by Medhātithi (on Manu
8. 3) – his views cited by Trikāṇḍamandana – he wrote bhāṣya on
Kātyāyana śrautasūtra and Pāraskara gṛhyasūtra – probably he
commented on Gautamadharmaśāstra – his explanation of Manu V.
143, Gaut. V. 41, XIV. 35–40, Vas. VIII. 1 quoted by Kalpataru –
probably commented on Pāraskaragṛhya – flourished about 800
A. D.
