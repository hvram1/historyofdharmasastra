=== CHAPTER ===
20. Gārgya

=== KANE'S SYNOPSIS ===
A sūtra work of Gārgya on dharma existed - Gārgya and
Vṛddha Gārgya - a Gārgīsamhitā on astronomy and astrology -
Jyotir Gārgya and Bṛhad Gārgya - Garga, an astronomer mention-
ed in Anuśāsana and Śalya probably different from the Dharma-
śāstra writer Garga - Vṛddha-Gārgya.
