Samvarta is prominently recognized as a dharmaśāstrakāra, notably mentioned as such by Yājñavalkya. His work was extensively cited across virtually all topics of dharma by eminent commentators such as Viśvarūpa, Medhātithi, and in the foundational text of the Mitākṣarā. Further attesting to his authority and widespread acceptance, Aparārka specifically quotes approximately 200 verses attributed to Samvarta.

Among his contributions, some of his views on *vyavahāra* are particularly noteworthy. The currently available printed version of the Saṁvarta text comprises around 230 verses. It is also important to recognize that his teachings circulated in various forms, as evidenced by references to both a Bṛhat Saṁvarta and a Svalpa Saṁvarta.
