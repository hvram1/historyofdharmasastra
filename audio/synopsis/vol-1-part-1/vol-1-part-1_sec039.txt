=== CHAPTER ===
39. Angiras

=== KANE'S SYNOPSIS ===
Quoted frequently on all topics except vyavahāra by writers from Viśvarūpa – Smṛticandrikā quotes some prose passages from Aṅgiras – several compilations on prāyaścitta attributed to Aṅgiras - Bṛhad-Aṅgiras and Madhyamāṅgiras – Aṅgiras-Smṛti in Ānand. Collection of Smṛtis has 168 verses – Aṅgirasasmṛti published by by Mr. A. N. Krishna Aiyangar contains over 1200 verses.
