Paithīnasi is identified as an ancient *sūtrakāra*, whose foundational teachings are notably preserved through Viśvarūpa's direct quotations of his *sūtras*. Scholarly inquiry, particularly by Dr. Jolly, further suggests that Paithīnasi's work may be associated with the Atharvaveda tradition, indicating a specific Vedic lineage.

His contributions to *Dharmaśāstra* are significant, delving into various critical aspects of Hindu law and custom. Paithīnasi's discussions encompass the intricate practice of *satī*, alongside comprehensive rules governing inheritance. Moreover, his writings offer unique perspectives on the concept of untouchability, specifically highlighting its absence under certain defined circumstances. Further elaboration on these subjects can also be found in Section 46.
