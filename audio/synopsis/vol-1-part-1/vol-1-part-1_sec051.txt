=== CHAPTER ===
51. Laugākshi

=== KANE'S SYNOPSIS ===
... pp.
Mitākṣarā quotes verses on āśauca and prāyaścitta, while
Aparārka quotes prose and verse passages on ācāra, āśauca, order
of forest-hermits - Laugākṣi's definition of yogakṣema quoted by
Mit. - quoted by Maskarin on Gant. Dh. S. as Lokākṣi.
