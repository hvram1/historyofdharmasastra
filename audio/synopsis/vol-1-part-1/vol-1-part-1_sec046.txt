Paithīnasi holds a significant position within the Dharmaśāstra tradition, being notably mentioned among the thirty-six Smṛtis cited by the esteemed commentator Aprārka. His work is remarkably comprehensive in its scope, addressing all three fundamental branches of Dharmaśāstra: ācāra, which pertains to righteous conduct and customs; vyavahāra, dealing with civil law and legal procedures; and prāyaścitta, concerning expiations and penances for transgressions.

An interesting stylistic characteristic of Paithīnasi's contributions is that prose quotations attributed to him considerably outnumber those in verse, suggesting a particular compositional approach that differentiates his text from some of the later, predominantly versified Smṛtis. The enduring authority and widespread recognition of Paithīnasi are further evidenced by the numerous passages quoted from his work by prominent legal commentators and digest writers, including the Mitākṣarā, Aprārka himself, and the Kalpataru, underscoring his foundational influence on subsequent legal thought.
