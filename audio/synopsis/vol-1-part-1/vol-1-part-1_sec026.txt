=== CHAPTER ===
26. Brihaspati

=== KANE'S SYNOPSIS ===
An ancient teacher of arthaśāstra mentioned in Kauṭilīya-
Mahābhārata ( Śānti 59. 80-85 ) credits him with compression of
vast work of Brahmā on trivarga and mentions several of his
views - Kāmasūtra speaks of Bṛhaspati as writer on artha - peculiar
views of Bṛhaspati according to Kauṭilīya - Bṛhaspati also wrote a
prose work on vyavahāra and peśayaścitta - probably the authors
of the two are different - 700 verses on vyavahāra ascribed to
Bṛhaspati are quoted in the Mitākṣarā - this is an independent
 
work composed between 300–500 A. D. – smaller compilations
in verse ascribed to Bṛhaspati - Bārhaspatya Arthaśāstra edited by
Dr. Thomas is a late work.
