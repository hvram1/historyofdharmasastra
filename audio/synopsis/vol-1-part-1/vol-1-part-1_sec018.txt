=== CHAPTER ===
18. Kanva and Kānva

=== KANE'S SYNOPSIS ===
Āp. Dh. S. ( I. 6. 19 ) shows that Kaṇva and Kāṇva were two
distinct authors - verses of Kaṇva quoted in Smṛticandrikā - Mit.
quotes a verse of Kāṇva - Maskarin frequently quotes passages
from Kaṇva.
