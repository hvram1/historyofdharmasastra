The chapter on Vishvāmitra begins by noting his recognition by Vṛddha Yājñavalkya, who quoted his verses on a wide array of topics, notably excluding *vyavahāra*. The *Smṛti* attributed to Vishvāmitra is estimated to have comprised a thousand verses, and its authority is further underscored by its mention in Parāśara M. at 7.28.

Vishvāmitra’s precepts touch upon fundamental aspects of *dharma*, including *saṁskāra*, *āśrama*, *vratas*, *śrāddha*, *dāna*, and *prāyaścitta*. Despite the purported thousand verses, only a limited number, such as fifty-four in *Smṛticandrikā* and fifty-nine in *Vyavahāramayūkha*, are found quoted. Interestingly, while not mentioned in early digests and commentaries like Medhātithi, his verses gained currency in later digests, including Aparārka, *Smṛticandrikā*, and *Caturvargacintāmaṇi*.

Further sources include a *Laghu Vishvāmitra Smṛti* comprising sixty verses, referred to simply as Vishvāmitra in *Harita Smṛti* 1. A clear distinction must be made between the *Vishvāmitra Dharmaśāstra* and the *Vishvāmitra Kalpasūtra*; the latter exclusively refers to sacrifices and not to *dharma*. It is important to note that no connection is established between the Vishvāmitra of the Purāṇas and the author of this particular *Smṛti*.
