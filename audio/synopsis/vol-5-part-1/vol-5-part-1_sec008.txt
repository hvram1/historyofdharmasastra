This chapter from P.V. Kane's monumental 'History of Dharmaśāstra' is dedicated to an exhaustive examination of *vratas*, which represent a pivotal category of religious observances within the Hindu tradition. The Dharmaśāstra literature provides extensive injunctions and intricate details regarding these practices, underscoring their profound significance in ritual, ethical, and devotional life across various strata of society.

Specifically, this chapter offers a meticulously researched exposition on four prominent *vratas*: Haritālikā, Gaṇeśacaturthī, Ṛṣipañcamī, and Anantacaturdaśī. Kane's characteristic scholarship meticulously explores the scriptural foundations, procedural intricacies, and cultural relevance of each of these important observances. The comprehensive treatment provides an authoritative account, elucidating the systematic approach to such religious disciplines within the expansive Dharmaśāstric framework, thus offering invaluable insights into their historical evolution and contemporary practice.
