This chapter delineates various individual *vratas*, delving into their specific observances and cultural significance within the Hindu tradition. We begin with *Caitra Pratipad*, marking the inception of the New Year in several regional calendars and signifying a period of renewed auspiciousness. Following closely is *Rāmanavamī*, a significant *vrata* that celebrates the auspicious birth of Śrī Rāma, embodying dharma and ideal kingship.

The discourse then moves to *Akṣayya-tṛtīyā*, an exceptionally propitious day believed to yield lasting merit for any good deed performed, often associated with new ventures and acts of charity. This period also encompasses *Paraśurāmajayantī*, commemorating the birth anniversary of the sixth incarnation of Vishnu. Further elaborating on significant observances, the chapter examines *Daśaharā*, particularly the Gangā Daśaharā, which reveres the descent of the sacred river Ganges to Earth.

Concluding this exploration of individual observances, a detailed exposition is provided for the *Sāvitrīvrata*, a deeply revered observance primarily undertaken by married women to pray for the longevity and prosperity of their husbands, drawing inspiration from the legendary devotion of Sāvitrī.
