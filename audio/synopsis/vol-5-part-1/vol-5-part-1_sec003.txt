This chapter meticulously outlines the qualifications of individuals entitled to engage in Vratas, specifying the diverse criteria for participation in these significant observances. It then delves into the various objects desired by means of Vratas, exploring the underlying motivations and spiritual aims that underpin their performance, whether for specific boons, purification, or the accumulation of religious merit consonant with dharma.

Subsequently, the text presents a comprehensive classification of Vratas, categorizing them based on their specific nature, duration, and intended outcomes. A significant portion is dedicated to the vast literature on Vratas, detailing the authoritative texts and scholarly works that meticulously expound upon their rules, procedures, and theological significance. Finally, the chapter elucidates the appropriate times for Vratas, addressing the auspicious periods, astrological considerations, and calendrical timings that dictate their proper execution, often linked to specific lunar phases, seasons, or sacred festivals.
