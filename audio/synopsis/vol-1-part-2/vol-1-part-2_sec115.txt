Nāgojibhaṭṭa was a scholar distinguished by a truly encyclopaedic learning, reflected in the standard works he authored across a remarkably diverse range of intellectual disciplines. These included significant contributions to grammar, dharmaśāstra, and yoga, among several other fields. Indeed, the meticulous research of Aufrecht attributes no fewer than forty-seven distinct compositions to Nāgojibhaṭṭa, a substantial portion of which were specifically dedicated to the intricate domain of dharmaśāstra.

Regarding his personal history, Nāgojibhaṭṭa enjoyed the patronage of Rāma, a prominent figure from the Bisen family. His scholarly lineage further highlights a crucial connection with the celebrated Bhaṭṭoji Dīkṣita, whose intellectual tradition he assiduously carried forward. Nāgojibhaṭṭa's prolific literary output was concentrated primarily within the first half of the eighteenth century, spanning the period between 1700 and 1750 A.D.
