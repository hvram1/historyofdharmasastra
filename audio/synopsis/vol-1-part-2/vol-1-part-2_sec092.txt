Harinātha is primarily recognized as the author of an influential digest, the *Smṛtisāra*. This scholarly work is notable for its meticulous approach, wherein Harinātha names and cites numerous authorities, thereby drawing upon a vast tradition of Dharmaśāstra scholarship. The contents of his *Smṛtisāra* are comprehensive, systematically exploring various facets of dharma, as is characteristic of such synthesising texts of Hindu law and custom.

From a chronological perspective, Harinātha is generally believed to have flourished sometime between 1300 and 1400 A.D., placing him firmly within the later medieval period of Dharmaśāstra literature. It is also pertinent to note that his *Smṛtisāra* is not singular in its appellation; rather, several other independent works from a similar period or genre were also styled *Smṛtisāra*, requiring careful scholarly discernment to avoid conflation and ensure accurate attribution.
