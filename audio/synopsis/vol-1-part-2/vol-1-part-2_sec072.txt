=== CHAPTER ===
72. Kāmadhenu

=== KANE'S SYNOPSIS ===
... pp.
An ancient digest not yet discovered – quoted by Kalpataru, Hāralatā, and other works – Gopāla, the author of Kāmadhenu – Aufrecht's view that Sambhu is the author of Kāmadhenu wrong – Sambhu is a nibandhakāra on dharma cited by Smṛticandrikā and Hemādri – Mr. Jayasval wrongly ascribes Kāmadhenu to Bhoja – probable date of Kāmadhenu between 1000-1100 A. D.
