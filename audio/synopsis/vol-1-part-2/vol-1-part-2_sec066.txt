=== CHAPTER ===
66. Devasvāmin

=== KANE'S SYNOPSIS ===
Said by Smṛticandrikā to have composed a digest of smṛtis – Nārāyaṇa, commentator of Āśvalāyanagṛhya, relies on bhāṣya of Devasvāmin – he composed a digest on ācāra, vyavahāra and āśauca – Smṛticandrikā quotes his views on the meaning of Yautaka, on the meaning of duhitarah in Yāj., on Manu 9. 141 – A Devasvāmin commented on Pūrvamīmāṁsāsūtras and on the
 
## Samkarṣakāṇḍa :
difficult to say whether he is identical with the writer on dharmaśāstra – Devasvāmin flourished about 1000–1050 A. D.
