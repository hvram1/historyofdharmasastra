=== CHAPTER ===
89. Kullūkabhatta

=== KANE'S SYNOPSIS ===
A famous commentator of Manusmṛti - he drew largely upon Medhātithi's bhāṣya and Govindarāja - Sir William Jones on Kullūka - authors and works quoted by him - personal history - he wrote Smṛtiviveka, of which Aśaucasāgara, Śrāddhasāgara and Vi- vādasāgara were parts - contents of Śrāddhasāgara - this is full of Pūrvamīmāṁsā discussions - date of Kullūka uncertain - flourished between 1150-1300 A. D.
