=== CHAPTER ===
88. Hemādri

=== KANE'S SYNOPSIS ===
He and Mādhava the two outstanding dākṣiṇātya writers on dharmaśāstra - his Caturvargacintāmaṇi is a huge work of an en- cyclopædic character - projected to contain five sections - printed parts comprise vrata, dāna, śrāddha and kāla - Hemādri a profound student of Pūrvamīmāṁsā - predecessors named by him - personal history of Hemādri - his connection with Yādavas of Devagiri - genealogy of the Yādavas - Caturvargacintāmaṇi composed about 1270 A. D. - com. on Śaunaka's Praṇavakalpa and a Śrāddhakalpa according to Kātyāyana are attributed to him - Vopadeva, a friend and a protege of Hemādri - references to Hemādri's work in grants.
