Kāshinātha Upādhyāya is primarily recognized for his extensive work titled Dharmasindhusāra. This treatise holds a pre-eminent position as a leading authority in the Deccan region, particularly concerning a vast array of religious matters and practices. Its scholarly depth is underscored by the meticulous enumeration of numerous authors and their works mentioned within its pages, which collectively address a broad spectrum of subjects relevant to dharma.

Beyond this significant opus, insights into Kāshinātha Upādhyāya's personal history reveal a prolific scholar. He is credited with authoring several other works, further contributing to the Dharmaśāstra tradition. Notably, the composition of the seminal Dharmasindhu, which refers to this same extensive work, can be precisely dated to the years 1790 and 1791 A.D., marking its definitive place in the chronology of Hindu legal and customary literature.
