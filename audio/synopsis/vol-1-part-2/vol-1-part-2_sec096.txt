=== CHAPTER ===
96. Vidyāpati

=== KANE'S SYNOPSIS ===
... pp.
Born in Biśapi, a village in north Bihar – fourth in descent from Dhīreśvara, uncle of Candeśvara – Love songs of Vidyāpati – credited with the authorship of 12 works – he was believed to be a great Vaiṣṇava, while in Mithilā he was held to be a Śaiva – he made Queen Viśvāsadevī’s work Gaṅgāvākyāvalī faultless and supplied textual authorities in support of her propositions – there appears to be agreement about the date of his death viz. 1448 A. D. – he may be held to have flourished between 1360–1448 A. D.
