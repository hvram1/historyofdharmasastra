During this significant epoch, the preparation of comprehensive digests became a notable endeavour, exemplified by one initiated under the patronage of Warren Hastings. This particular digest saw the development of both Persian and English renderings, facilitating wider access and understanding. In a similar vein, the *Vivādasarārnava* was meticulously compiled by Trivedi Sarvoruśarman.

Nevertheless, amongst the numerous digests meticulously brought together under the direct influence of the British administration, the *Vivādabhaṅgārṇava* by Jagannātha Tarkapanchānana emerged as the most widely recognized and esteemed. The profound significance of this work was further amplified by its translation into English by Colebrooke in 1796. The *Vivādabhaṅgārṇava* comprehensively addresses and expounds upon a multitude of critical topics within the realm of Dharmaśāstra. It is important to note that Jagannātha Tarkapanchānana, the esteemed author of this influential compilation, passed away in the year 1806.
