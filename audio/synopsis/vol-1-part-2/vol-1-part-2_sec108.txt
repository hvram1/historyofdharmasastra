Nārāyaṇabhaṭṭa is recognized as the most distinguished member of the Bhaṭṭa family, originating from Benares. A glimpse into his personal history reveals his birth in the year 1513 A.D., marking the commencement of a life dedicated to scholarship.

His substantial literary legacy encompasses several important works. Prominent among these are the *Antyeṣṭipaddhati*, a treatise on funeral rites, the *Tristhalisetu*, likely pertaining to holy places or pilgrimage, and the *Prayogaratna*, which elucidates various religious practices. Furthermore, Nārāyaṇabhaṭṭa authored a significant commentary on verses found in *Kālamādhava*. The span of his principal literary activity is documented between the years 1540 and 1570 A.D., reflecting a productive three decades of scholarly output.

However, it is essential to draw a clear distinction regarding the authorship of *Dharmapravṛtti*; the Nārāyaṇa responsible for this work is a different individual and should not be conflated with the Nārāyaṇabhaṭṭa who is the subject of this overview.
