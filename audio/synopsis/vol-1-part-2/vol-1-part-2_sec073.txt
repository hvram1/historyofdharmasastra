The name Halāyudha refers to several distinct individuals. The earliest among them authored the Kavirahasya and Mṛtasañjīvanī, flourishing in the latter half of the tenth century. The Kavirahasya, in particular, presents several interesting features.

Another significant figure bearing this name was the author of the Brāhmana-sarvasva, alongside four other works. His personal history reveals patronage by Laksmanasena, the king of Bengal, placing his literary activity squarely within the period of 1160 to 1200 A.D. Notably, there is voluminous literature associated with this Laksmanasena and his era (La. Saṁ.). It is crucial to distinguish this Halāyudha from another who was a jurist, known for composing a work titled 'Vivāda'. We also encounter yet another Halāyudha, credited with a commentary named Prakāśa on Kātyāyana's Śrāddhakalpasūtra, whose floruit must fall between 1150 A.D. and 1509 A.D. Lastly, there is Halāyudha, the author of the Karmopadeśinī, whose existence is dated earlier than the fifteenth century.
