Todarānanda stands as an extensive encyclopaedia encompassing religious law, astronomy, and medicine. Significant portions of this work, specifically two parts on Sarga and Avatāras, have been published by Dr. P. L. Vaidya. This monumental compilation is intimately connected with Toḍarmal, whose family and ancestors are also discussed in relation to its origins. The Todarānanda is meticulously structured into twenty-two distinct sections, known as saukhyas, each of which is further subdivided into harṣas.

Its composition spanned a considerable period, from 1572 A.D. to 1589 A.D., concluding in the very year of Toḍarmal’s demise. The chapter provides an account of the work's overall extent, alongside details of some of these saukhyas, with particular mention of the Jyotiḥsaukhya, which was completed in 1572 A.D. Additionally, other relevant works useful for understanding the history of Toḍaramalla are noted.

Regarding its authorship, Dr. Vaidya's ascription of the Todarānanda chiefly to Nārāyaṇabhaṭṭa is presented as not acceptable. Instead, the specific authorship of the Jyotiḥsaukhya, for instance, is attributed to Nīlakaṇṭha.
